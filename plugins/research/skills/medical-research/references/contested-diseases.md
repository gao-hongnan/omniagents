# Contested & Emerging Diseases

For long COVID, ME/CFS, POTS/dysautonomia, fibromyalgia, and MCAS, the
ordinary evidence hierarchy is necessary but not sufficient. These conditions
are fast-moving, lack validated biomarkers, have competing case definitions,
and carry a history of clinical neglect. Apply this lens whenever a question
touches them.

## Why textbooks fail here

- **Stale**: the field turns over faster than textbook revision cycles; some
  still recommend graded exercise therapy, contraindicated for ME/CFS since
  NICE NG206 (2021).
- **Contested case definitions**: "ME/CFS" under Oxford (fatigue only) is a
  different population than under IOM 2015 (requires post-exertional malaise).
  Findings do not transfer across definitions — always record which was used.
- **No validated biomarker**: diagnoses are clinical; many studies are small
  and heterogeneous.

## Where the leading evidence actually lives

Weight these **up** (while still appraising method):

- **Large cohorts / GWAS**: RECOVER (long COVID), DecodeME (ME/CFS genetics).
- **Preprints** (medRxiv): often months ahead of journals — cite, but flag
  not-yet-peer-reviewed and down-tier.
- **Named labs / programs**: e.g. Iwasaki (immunology), Putrino (clinical),
  Davis/Phair (metabolic) — useful for tracing credible mechanistic work.
- **Rigorous patient-led research**: the Patient-Led Research Collaborative and
  #MEAction surfaced post-exertional malaise harms and helped correct the GET
  record. Appraise sampling/method, but do not dismiss — this is a field where
  patient-collected data has repeatedly been ahead of the literature
  (epistemic injustice toward these patients is well documented).

## The five live mechanisms (non-exclusive)

Seed hypotheses against these mapped-to-subtype mechanisms; do not crown one
winner — they overlap across long COVID / ME-CFS / POTS / fibro / MCAS:

1. **Viral persistence** (antigen/RNA reservoirs).
2. **Autoimmunity / autoantibodies** (e.g. GPCR autoantibodies).
3. **Immune dysregulation + herpesvirus reactivation** (EBV/HHV-6).
4. **Dysautonomia / POTS** (autonomic dysfunction).
5. **Mitochondrial / metabolic** dysfunction.

## Traps to avoid

- **Outcome-switched unblindable trials** — PACE (ME/CFS): reported recovery
  collapsed on reanalysis; treat self-report-only behavioral-intervention RCTs
  with suspicion and demand objective endpoints.
- **Single-lab striking findings without replication** — the microclot story:
  compelling early data, uneven independent replication. Require standardized
  assays before tiering up.
- **Predatory / harmful interventions** — apheresis ("blood-washing"),
  stem-cell/exosome clinics, ozone therapy, and brain-retraining programs
  (Lightning Process, DNRS). Document harms (infection, blindness, death in
  reported cases) and price ($3k–25k). Surface these in `safety_flags`; never
  relay them neutrally as options.
- **Graded exercise therapy / "push through"** — exertion can cause lasting
  harm via post-exertional malaise. Never present GET as recommended.

## What makes evidence credible here

- Uses a PEM-requiring case definition (for ME/CFS) and says so.
- Objective endpoints, pre-registered, no recovery redefinition.
- Replicated by an independent lab with a standardized assay.
- Declares (or visibly lacks) commercial conflict.
- For preprints: flagged as such and weighted accordingly.
