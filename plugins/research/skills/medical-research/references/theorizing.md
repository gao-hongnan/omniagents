# The Theorizing Engine (hypothesis mode)

Active **only in hypothesis mode**, behind the hard wall from the grounded
channel. The point is to go beyond the textbook **without inventing facts**.
Novelty comes from the *combinatorics of grounded facts* — never from a
fabricated edge.

## Generation — Swanson ABC literature-based discovery

Take two relations from **disconnected** grounded sources and propose the
missing link:

- Source 1 asserts **A → B** (cited).
- Source 2 asserts **B → C** (cited).
- Hypothesis: **A → C**, via the bridging concept **B**.

The novelty is the *connection*; both edges stay cited. **Name B explicitly**
so the output is a mechanistic chain with directional verbs
(`inhibits`/`elevates`/`drives`/`triggers`), not a vague "A is linked to C".
This is the proven novelty-without-fabrication engine — Swanson's
fish-oil→Raynaud's and magnesium→migraine were later confirmed, every link
literature-grounded.

In the schema, each `chain` edge must reference a `citation` index whose source
states that edge. An uncited edge is a fabricated fact and is rejected.

## Discipline — Strong Inference + multiple working hypotheses

Never advance a single pet mechanism. For each observation:

1. Generate **2–4 competing explanations** (the `competing` field; ≥1
   required).
2. Propose the **discriminating test** — the experiment or observation that
   would *exclude* one or more competitors (not merely confirm your favourite).
3. State the **falsification** — the specific result that would refute the
   hypothesis.

That move converts a "plausible story" into a scientific hypothesis. Scaffold
abduction explicitly: enumerate the alternatives and what evidence each
requires, rather than asserting a single "best explanation".

## Novelty check (measure, don't assert)

Before badging a hypothesis `novelty: "novel"`, verify the A→C connection is
**absent** from the retrieved sources. If the link already exists in the
literature, it is `"established"` and belongs as a grounded claim with a
citation — not a novel hypothesis. This prevents both false-novelty and
accidental rediscovery of textbook facts.

## Anchor every speculation to a grounded fact

Build each mechanistic chain outward from ≥1 grounded anchor. Bundle every
hypothesis with:

- its confidence (low by construction — these are SPECULATIVE),
- its **assumptions** (each leap the chain takes that the sources do not
  state),
- the **confirmatory + discriminating experiment** that would upgrade or kill
  it.

Untethered speculation is the failure mode; tethered, tiered, testable
speculation is responsible theorizing.

## Verify hypotheses harder than you generate them

(The AI-co-scientist lesson: spend most compute on verification.) The
`hypothesis-critic` agent runs an adversarial pass on every hypothesis:

- confirm each `chain` edge actually resolves to a source that asserts it;
- challenge the mechanism's logic and flag any edge that does not hold;
- guard against spurious ABC bridges — a high-frequency but meaningless
  bridging term B (e.g. "inflammation" connecting everything) is not a
  mechanism. Require relation specificity and a plausible pathway.
- rank surviving hypotheses; surface only those that pass.

A hypothesis that fails the critic is dropped or demoted to an open question —
never promoted into the grounded `claims`.
